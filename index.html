Model & assumptions

Each item’s peak is its maximum quarterly demand across five years. A price-group candidate sets s = ceil(M × peak) and S = s + Q. The browser simulates shared resampled demand scenarios, then selects one candidate per group to minimize inventory value under workload and wait limits.

Synthetic prices, intermittent demand, one-to-two-quarter lead times, and one purchase per simulated replenishment are demonstration assumptions. The wait proxy uses quarterly increments. The forecast baseline uses a one-standard-deviation safety term and a four-quarter coverage quantity. This prototype addresses Peak Policy only and excludes Appendix B Next Gen.

Small-case verification

Six deterministic items across two price groups. Expected candidate costs, workload, fills, waits, the forecast baseline, and binary optimization outcomes are checked in the browser.
